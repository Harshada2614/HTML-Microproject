Safety Tips





Women safety is an important aspect of society. Being aware of your surroundings,
avoiding unsafe places, and trusting your instincts can prevent danger. Always
share your location with trusted people and keep emergency numbers ready.



Situation Action
Walking Alone Stay alert, avoid headphones
Public Transport Stay near crowd & exits
Emergency Call 112 immediately

Self Defense Techniques



Wrist Escape

Twist your wrist towards the attacker’s thumb and pull away quickly.

Elbow Strike

Use your elbow to strike when attacker is close.

Knee Attack

Use your knee to hit attacker’s lower body for escape.

Women Support NGOs

Many organizations work towards women safety, empowerment, and justice. They provide legal help, shelter, and awareness programs.

  • SEWA - Self Employed Women's Association
  • Akshara Centre
  • Jagori

These organizations help women become independent and raise awareness about their rights. Supporting such initiatives builds a safer society.
